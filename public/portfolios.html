Muffin Time 吸爆鬆餅

Muffin Time is a chaotic card game with more twists and turns than you can shake a spork at! Battle your friends, family, and hyper-intelligent pets by drawing and playing from a deck of unique cards that'll either help you, thwart others, or mix things up for the sheer hell of it! To win the game, you just need to start your turn with exactly 10 cards in your hand but if you think that'll be easy, you're wrong (wronger than a duck wearing shoes- and that's pretty damn wrong).

這款派對遊戲的美術是由Thomas Ridgewell操刀，也就是無厘頭到備受讚譽的「asdfmovie」作者，在原系列短片中那不可預期的隨機性，與懷疑作者該去看醫生的超展開都化作遊戲卡牌忠實呈現！遊戲的卡牌分為「藍色」行動卡、「綠色」反擊卡和「紅色」陷阱卡，卡上的能力都圍繞著拿取卡牌或令其他玩家棄掉手牌，玩家需要善用卡牌在一團混戰中取得剛好10張手牌來獲得最終勝利。

About Unizza Games

Our name combines the prefix of unique “uni-” with “-zza” from pizza — a playful image of the gaming world. The name also subtly reflects our Unity-based development origins. As we evolved to focus more on publishing, localization, and game licensing. Unizza Games became the perfect identity to capture our expanded vision.
