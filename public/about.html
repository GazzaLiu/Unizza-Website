About Us

Taiwan-based, working across Asia and worldwide.

Who We Are

At Unizza Games, we specialize in connecting the global board game community with the best sourcing solutions in Asia. Whether you're looking to expand your game into new markets, localize it for different audiences, or transform it into an exciting digital experience, we’ve got you covered. With our deep expertise and passion for games, we ensure every project stands out from the rest.

Our Team

Our Mission

Great games deserve players everywhere. We help publishers and designers bring their board games to Asia — and bring Asian games to the world — with care for every card, rulebook and box.

Why "Unizza"?

Our name combines the prefix of unique “uni-” with “-zza” from pizza — a playful image of the gaming world. The name also subtly reflects our Unity-based development origins. As we evolved to focus more on publishing, localization, and game licensing. Unizza Games became the perfect identity to capture our expanded vision.
